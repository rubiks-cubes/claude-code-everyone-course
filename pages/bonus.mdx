## The Gauntlet 🏆

> **Run it:** Type `/start-bonus` in Claude Code

**Prerequisites:** Module 1 (agents from lesson 1.6) + Module 2 (the quiz). Module 3 is optional.

---

## What Is This?

One prompt. Three agents. Full verdict on your quiz.

You type a single prompt. Three agents you built in lesson 1.6 launch simultaneously — each reviewing your Coffee Personality Quiz from a completely different angle. Claude synthesizes their feedback into a final verdict. You pick one thing to fix and ship it.

No setup. No new tools. Pure multi-agent orchestration from a single command.

## The Three Agents

| Agent | Persona | Their angle |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| **(•‿•) Barista Lead** | Hears customers talk about coffee every day | "Does this feel like a real coffee shop? Would our regulars take this?" |
| **(ಠ_ಠ) Exec** | Needs numbers and business outcomes | "What's the projected lift in loyalty sign-ups? What would I track?" |
| **(◠‿◠) Product Designer** | Focused on emotion and delight | "Where's the moment of magic? Would someone share their result?" |

## The Prompt

Copy this verbatim into Claude Code:

```
Use the Barista Lead, Exec, and Product Designer agents simultaneously to review
the Coffee Personality Quiz at @quiz-project. I want each agent to give their
honest assessment — what works, what doesn't, and one thing they'd change.
Then synthesize all three reviews into a final verdict and save it to
analysis/gauntlet-report.md
```

## What Happens

1. All three agents launch **in parallel** — watch them all go at once
2. Each returns an in-character assessment
3. Claude synthesizes all three into a structured final verdict
4. The report is saved to `analysis/gauntlet-report.md`

## The Report Structure

```markdown
# Gauntlet Report — Coffee Personality Quiz

## The Panel
- Barista Lead: [their take in 1-2 sentences]
- Exec: [their take in 1-2 sentences]
- Product Designer: [their take in 1-2 sentences]

## What's Working
## What Needs Work
## The One Fix That Matters Most
## Final Verdict
```

## After the Report

Read it. All of it.

Then pick **one thing** — the highest-impact change from the verdict — and fix it. Push to GitHub. Done.

That's the whole lesson. One fix, shipped.

## Why One Fix?

Overwhelm is the enemy of shipping. Real product development isn't about fixing everything at once — it's about picking the most important thing and doing it.

The Gauntlet ends when you've shipped the fix. Not when you've read the report.

## The Bigger Point

You just ran a multi-agent review process:
- Three independent perspectives
- One synthesized output
- One concrete action
- No meeting, no email thread, no waiting for feedback

This scales to anything:
- A landing page before you launch it
- A business proposal before you send it
- A job description before you post it
- A process doc nobody reads

Same pattern. Different agents. Different context.
